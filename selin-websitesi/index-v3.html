Documentary · 2025

Siyah Kalem

An artist whose name is not even fully known pushed the boundaries of imagination in 15th-century Central Asia by telling stories of jinn, giants, and demons, just to vanish right after. He left behind only scattered, fragmented paintings that seemed unrelated to each other. We do not know who he was, nor what he thought or intended to convey. This creative effort is a quest to decipher the meaning his creations—now ours to interact and echo—evoke in our minds.

Short Film · 2022

Suicide Watchman

In response to the dramatic rise in suicide cases in recent months, the Ministry of Interior issues a nationwide directive. Under the new policy, neighbourhood watchmen are assigned to take shifts on bridges to prevent suicide attempts, and the Ministry will reward them for every life they save. Raşit is posted to a remote, unnamed, and crumbling bridge in a village far from the city centre, crossed by no one but sheep. The people around him constantly mock his place of duty. As a result, one of the deepest impulses in human nature—the desire for recognition—begins to take hold of him.

Documentary · 2024

Arabesk Rap: Bir Neslin Sesi - Voice of a Generation: Rap Alaturka

Rap Alaturca explores the phenomenon of Arabesk Rap, a kind of synthesis of rap music, which has attracted great attention around the world, and arabesk music, which has been widely followed in Türkiye since the 1970s. In the documentary, one can find a variety of interviews with acclaimed Turkish immigrant musicians in Germany from the 90s, including artists who reflect street culture through rap music and musicians who are famous for their arabesque rap performances. The documentary delves into the studios, homes, and music video sets of arabesque rap artists, who have garnered millions of fans in Türkiye recently, particularly on social media. The documentary searches the phenomenon of arabesque rap through the perspective of its popular figures, telling the genre's story to viewers in a dynamic and fast-paced language that matches its energy.

Short Film in Production · 2026

Mother, Will Dad Be Angry with Me?

Hacer is called to school by her daughter’s newly appointed teacher. Expecting a routine meeting, she instead finds herself facing an unsettling accusation: the handwriting in her daughter Esma’s notebook does not match the writing she produces in class. For Hacer, the situation is not simply about homework. Illiterate and shaped by a life defined by strict moral rules, she believes that protecting her daughter’s future is her greatest responsibility. Yet when the teacher openly questions whether Esma’s homework is truly her own, the conversation turns into a confrontation that challenges Hacer’s sense of dignity and truth. As the accusation of lying begins to echo beyond the classroom, the fragile balance within the family starts to crack. Determined to defend herself and protect her daughter, Hacer’s actions slowly place an increasing pressure on the child. Within the quiet routines of everyday life, Esma becomes the silent carrier of her mother’s fears and expectations. What begins as a mother’s attempt to do the right thing gradually reveals how love, authority, and fear can intertwine—turning care into an invisible form of violence.
